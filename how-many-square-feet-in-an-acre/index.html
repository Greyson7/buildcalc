How Many Square Feet in an Acre?

The conversion you Google every time you read a real-estate listing or shop a land parcel. Here is the number, the visualization, and the related conversions.

Quick answer: 1 acre = 43,560 square feet = a square ~209 × 209 ft. About the size of an American football field minus the end zones.

Where 43,560 comes from

It is a historical British unit, originally defined as the area a yoke of oxen could plow in one day. The modern statute number:

  1. 1 chain = 66 ft (a surveyor's chain).
  2. 1 furlong = 10 chains = 660 ft.
  3. 1 acre = 1 chain × 1 furlong = 66 × 660 = 43,560 sq ft.
  4. As a square: √43,560 = 208.71 ft per side.

  • 1 square mile = 640 acres = 27,878,400 sq ft.

Where this comes up

  • Real estate — listing acreage to compare against lot dimensions in feet.
  • Landscaping — figuring fertilizer, seed or chemical coverage rates (sold per sq ft, lot measured in acres).
  • Land surveys — old deeds measured in chains, links and acres; you need to convert for modern math.
  • Agriculture — yield measured per acre, machinery rated per acre per hour.

FAQ

How many square feet are in one acre?+

One acre is exactly 43,560 square feet. The number comes from the historical British definition: 1 acre = 10 square chains = 10 × (66 ft)² = 43,560 sq ft.

What does one acre look like?+

A square acre is 208.71 feet on each side — slightly smaller than a 220 × 220 backyard. A standard American football field including both end zones is 1.32 acres. The playing field alone (between end zones) is 1.1 acres.

How many acres are in a square mile?+

640 acres in one square mile. A standard rural land "section" in the U.S. Public Land Survey System is one square mile = 640 acres. A "quarter section" is 160 acres — the famous Homestead Act allotment.

How many acres are in a hectare?+

One hectare is about 2.471 acres. Or in reverse: 1 acre = 0.4047 hectares. A hectare is 10,000 square meters; an acre is 4,046.86 square meters.

How big is half an acre?+

Half an acre is 21,780 square feet — roughly a square 147 feet on each side, or a rectangle about 100 × 218 feet. That is generous suburban-lot size in most U.S. neighborhoods.

How big is a quarter acre?+

A quarter acre is 10,890 square feet — about a 104 × 104 ft square, or a 100 × 110 ft rectangle. That is the typical suburban-lot size in most American developments.
